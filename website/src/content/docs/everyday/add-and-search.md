title: Add, search and shortcuts
description: The add menu, search everything, and the keyboard shortcuts that make Arca quick.
---

Two things are always in reach, wherever you are in Arca: the **+** to add something and **Search everything**.

## The add menu

The round **+** next to the search field in the sidebar (the orange **+** in the middle of the tab bar on a phone) opens the add menu:

- **Transaction**: an expense, income or transfer.
- **Recurring bill**: something that comes back every month, quarter or year.
- **Debt**: something you owe, or something someone owes you.
- **Goal**: save towards something, or cap a category.
- **Category**: a new category for your transactions.

Each one opens right where you are, so you don't lose your place. On a computer, <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd> on a Mac) opens the menu too.

## Search everything

**Search everything** in the sidebar (the magnifier at the end of the tab bar on a phone) searches the whole app, not just the page you're on. It finds:

- transactions, by their description or the original bank text,
- categories,
- fixed costs (recurring items),
- debts.

Before you type, it shows quick actions such as **Add transaction**, **Import CSV**, **Go to Budget** and **Go to Insights**. Use <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> to move through the results and <kbd>Enter</kbd> to open one.

:::note
Activity also has its own search box. That one only filters the transaction list on that page.
:::

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Keys | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> | Open search from anywhere |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>N</kbd> | Open the add menu |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> | Move through Activity; the open transaction follows |
| <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> | Switch between Summary and Details of a transaction |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close what's on top, or leave selection mode |
| Number keys | Type amounts straight into the keypad; <kbd>Enter</kbd> saves |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> / <kbd>S</kbd> | In Needs a category: save and next / skip |

In date pickers, the arrow keys move between days and <kbd>Enter</kbd> picks one.
