---
title: Upcoming
description: Every bill, subscription, debt payment and expected income in a month.
---

**Upcoming** shows what's due this month: bills, subscriptions, debt payments and the income you expect, as a list and a calendar.

## What you see

- At the top, what's **still to leave before payday**: the number of bills, their total and what's left in your account after them, next to your next income.
- **Balance through the month**: how your current account moves bill by bill, counting bills and income only. Set its balance once to see it.
- A **calendar** of the month with an amount on every day that has money moving, marked **Paid**, **To pay**, **Income**, **Today** and your payday. Tap a day for its bills.
- **Still due**: the bills not paid yet, grouped as **Overdue**, **This week** and **Later this month**, with the amount over each bill's status. Paid ones fold away underneath. Once everything is paid, it says which bill is next.
- A **preview** of next month: what it brings in bills, compared with this month.

## Look back or ahead

Use the arrows to see an earlier month (which bills were paid) or the next one (what's coming). You can go two years back and a year ahead.

## Paid or not

A bill is ticked off by itself when a matching transaction comes in: it then says **Paid — matched to a transaction**. Each due date takes the matching payment closest to it, also when that payment lands just before payday or just after, and one payment never pays two bills.

Banks don't collect on Saturday or Sunday, so a bill due on a weekend is shown on the Monday after (income keeps its own date).

If you paid some other way, use **Mark as paid**. A bill you marked yourself says **Marked by you**, and no longer counts against [Safe to spend](/Arca/everyday/home/#safe-to-spend). **Mark as unpaid** puts it back.

:::tip[Bills come from your recurring items]
Upcoming is built from your [recurring items](/Arca/plan/recurring/). **Add a bill** creates a new one; **Recurring items** opens the full list.
:::
