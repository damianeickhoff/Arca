title: Upcoming
description: Every bill, subscription, debt payment and expected income in a month.
---

**Upcoming** shows what's due this month: bills, subscriptions, debt payments and the income you expect, as a list and a calendar.

## What you see

- Three tiles: **Still due**, **Paid** and **Expected in**.
- Lists of what's **Still due**, what's **Paid** and the **Income expected**.
- A calendar with every day that has money moving, marked **Paid**, **To pay**, **Income** and **Today**, and your payday. Tap a day for its bills.

## Look back or ahead

Use the arrows to see an earlier month (which bills were paid) or the next one (what's coming). You can go two years back and a year ahead.

## Paid or not

A bill is ticked off by itself when a matching transaction comes in: it then says **Paid — matched to a transaction**.

If you paid some other way, use **Mark as paid**. A bill you marked yourself says **Marked by you**, and no longer counts against [Safe to spend](/Arca/everyday/home/#safe-to-spend). **Mark as unpaid** puts it back.

:::tip[Bills come from your recurring items]
Upcoming is built from your [recurring items](/Arca/plan/recurring/). **Add a bill** creates a new one; **Recurring items** opens the full list.
:::
