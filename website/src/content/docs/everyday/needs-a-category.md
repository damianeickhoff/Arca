description: Sort uncategorised transactions one by one, and teach Arca so the list stays short.

**Needs a category** is the queue of transactions Arca couldn't file by itself. Sort them here, and turn each answer into a rule so next month's list is shorter.

## Why transactions end up here

A transaction lands in the queue when no rule, merchant or recurring item gave it a category. Internal transfers and split transactions never do. Until it has a category, a transaction is **left out of budgets** and insights, so it's worth keeping the queue short.

You get here from the **Review** strip on Home, the count in Activity, or the badge on Activity in the phone's tab bar.

## Sort one at a time

You see one transaction at a time, with the text exactly as it appears on your bank statement.

1. Pick a category. **Suggested** ones are at the top, based on your rules and earlier choices; **All categories** are below.
2. Choose **Save and next** (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>).
3. Not sure? **Skip** (or <kbd>S</kbd>) moves on without saving. **Mark as internal transfer** takes it out of your spending altogether.

Switch on **Also create a rule for similar transactions** and Arca files the matching ones too, now and on every import.

## Teach it once

After you file a transaction without a rule, Arca asks **Always file … as …?**

- **Always** files this merchant the same way from now on, and moves the earlier ones too.
- **Just this one** only files this transaction.
- **Edit match** lets you change what Arca looks for before you save.

More about this in [Rules and merchants](/Arca/transactions/rules-and-merchants/).

## Worth teaching

Merchants with a pile of transactions waiting show up under **Worth teaching**. File one, tap **Always**, and the rest are sorted at once. This is the quickest way to empty a long queue after your first import.

## Sort several at once

Tap **Select** to pick several transactions from the queue and give them one category, exclude them, or delete them in one go.

## Ask AI

If you've switched on [AI](/Arca/settings/ai/), an **Ask AI** button suggests categories for this transaction and the next ones in the queue. The AI's pick is marked **Suggested by AI**; you still choose.

:::tip
When the queue is empty you'll see **Nothing to sort**. That's the goal.
:::
