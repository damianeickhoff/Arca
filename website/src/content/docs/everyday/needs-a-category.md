description: Sort uncategorised transactions one by one, and teach Arca so the list stays short.

**Needs a category** is the queue of transactions Arca couldn't file by itself. Sort them here, and turn each answer into a rule so next month's list is shorter.

## Why transactions end up here

A transaction lands in the queue when no rule, merchant or recurring item gave it a category. Internal transfers and split transactions never do. Until it has a category, a transaction is **left out of budgets** and insights, so it's worth keeping the queue short.

You get here from the **Review** strip on Home, the count in Activity, or the badge on Activity in the phone's tab bar.

## Sort one at a time

You see one transaction at a time, with the text exactly as it appears on your bank statement.

1. Pick a category. **Suggested** ones are at the top, based on your rules and earlier choices; **All categories** are below.
2. Choose **Save and next** (or press <kbd>Enter</kbd>).
3. Not sure? **Skip** (or <kbd>S</kbd>) moves on without saving.

## Internal transfer

Money you moved to one of your own accounts isn't spending. Arca spots this by itself when both accounts are in Arca, but not every account can be added. For those, pick **Internal transfer** beside the suggestions and choose **Save and next**. The transaction leaves the queue and stays out of your budgets and spending, just like the transfers Arca finds itself. You can undo it in the transaction's details.

## Also create a rule

Switch on **Also create a rule for similar transactions** and Arca files the matching ones too, now and on every import.

Want to know which ones first? **Show the N transactions** lists every earlier transaction the rule would file now, with date, description, amount and account. They're all ticked; untick the ones the rule should leave alone, or use **Select none** and tick a few. Unticked transactions keep what they have now, also when rules run again later. The rule itself stays and files new transactions that match.

## Teach it once

After you file a transaction without a rule, Arca asks **Always file … as …?**

- **Always** files this merchant the same way from now on, and moves the earlier ones too.
- **Just this one** only files this transaction.
- **Show the N transactions** lists what moves, so you can untick the ones to leave alone.
- **Edit match** lets you change what Arca looks for before you save.

More about this in [Rules and merchants](/Arca/transactions/rules-and-merchants/).

## Worth teaching

Merchants with a pile of transactions waiting show up under **Worth teaching**. File one, tap **Always**, and the rest are sorted at once. This is the quickest way to empty a long queue after your first import.

Is the merchant really your own other account? Pick **Internal transfer** in the list. Arca asks whether to mark all of that merchant's waiting transactions or just this one.

## Sort several at once

Tap **Select** to pick several transactions from the queue and give them one category, exclude them, or delete them in one go. You can also hold a row (phone) or Shift-click it (computer) to start selecting.

Picked several from different shops that all belong in, say, Groceries? Choose **Category**, pick it, and switch on **Also create a rule for each of the merchants**. Arca makes one rule per merchant, the same rule it makes for a single transaction. **Show the N transactions** then lists what those rules file now, grouped per merchant, so you can untick a whole merchant or single transactions.

## Ask AI

If you've switched on [AI](/Arca/settings/ai/), an **Ask AI** button suggests categories for this transaction and the next ones in the queue. The AI's pick is marked **Suggested by AI**; you still choose.

Arca keeps the AI's picks with the transactions. Reload the page or come back tomorrow and they're still there, and Arca doesn't ask the AI again for them. A pick goes away once the transaction has a category.

:::tip
When the queue is empty you'll see **Nothing to sort**. That's the goal.
:::
