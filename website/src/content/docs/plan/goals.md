title: Goals

**Plan → Goals** helps you save towards something (a buffer, a holiday, a new laptop) and shows the month you get there.

## Two kinds of goal

- **Savings goal**: an amount you want to have.
- **Budget goal**: a cap on what you spend in one category.

## Add a goal

Use the **+** add menu → **Goal**, or start from a suggestion: **Emergency buffer**, **Holiday** or **New laptop**. **Use this** fills in a name, icon and colour; change anything before you save.

Fill in:

- **Name**, icon and colour.
- **Target amount** and what you already have (**Starting balance**).
- How much you put aside and **how often you save**: weekly, monthly or yearly.
- Optionally a **Category**, a **Start date** and an **End date**. With an end date, Arca works out how much you need to save each time.

## Saving towards it

You add to a goal by linking transactions to it. Open the transaction that moved money to your savings in [Activity](/Arca/everyday/activity/), and pick the goal under **Savings goal**.

- Money going out (to savings) adds to the goal.
- Money coming back in (from savings) is taken off it.

The **Contributions** chart shows twelve months of what you put aside, with this month as the bright bar.

## When you get there

Open a goal for its history and the month you reach it at your current pace: "At €150 a month you reach €3,000 in June 2027". Without a regular amount there's no date yet. Once the target is reached, it says when.
