Recurring items are the things that come back: your salary, rent, insurance, subscriptions, a savings transfer. Arca matches them to your transactions, ticks them off when they're paid, and uses them for your budget, [Upcoming](/Arca/everyday/upcoming/) and the [forecast](/Arca/insights/forecast/).

## Add one

Use the **+** add menu → **Recurring bill**, **Add a bill** on Upcoming, or **Make recurring** on a transaction. All recurring items are listed in Settings → **Recurring**, with filters for income, bills, subscriptions, debts, savings and payment plans.

## Types

| Type | For |
| --- | --- |
| **Income** | Your salary, benefits, rent you receive |
| **Bill** | Rent, energy, insurance, phone |
| **Subscription** | Streaming, the gym, software |
| **Debt** | Repayments on a loan (see [Debts](/Arca/plan/debts/)) |
| **Savings** | A regular transfer to savings |
| **Payment plan** | One cost paid in parts (below) |

Each item has an amount, a **frequency** (once, daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly), a start date and optionally an end date. You can also give it a category, a **budget type**, a **friendly name** (shown on matched transactions instead of the bank's name) and notes. Switch off **Active** (or tap **Pause** in its details) to pause it.

Paused items and items past their end date move to **Archived** at the bottom of **Settings › Recurring**, so the list only shows what's running. They keep their charges there, and you can open one to resume or edit it.

## Matching to your transactions

Arca ticks a recurring item off when a transaction matches it:

- **Match pattern**: text that appears in the bank's description, for example "VATTENFALL". Switch on **Whole word only** so a short pattern doesn't catch longer names it happens to sit inside.
- **Another name**: banks sometimes rename a payee. Tap **Add another name** to match the old and the new name, so every charge stays linked to the item.
- **Match amount**: an **exact amount**, an amount **between** two numbers (for bills that vary), or **any amount**.

As you type, a chart shows which past charges the item would pick up, so you can check before you save. A matched transaction gets the recurring item's category.

The item's details show its last 12 charges, and call it out when the price changes. A long gap (a subscription you stopped and started again) shows as a dashed line and doesn't count as a price change. When the charges span more than one year, the years are shown under the bars.

## Found by Arca

Arca looks for payments that come back by themselves at a steady rhythm and amount, after every import or bank sync (or for your whole history from **Settings › Data health › Look for regular payments**). What it finds shows up as a suggestion on Upcoming and in **Settings › Recurring**, well-known subscriptions first. Nothing is added until you review it: **Accept** adds it as a recurring item, **Edit** lets you change it first, and **Not a bill** makes sure it isn't suggested again.

## Payment plans

A **payment plan** is one cost you pay in parts, like the dentist in three instalments or a new phone over twelve months.

1. Choose the type **Payment plan**.
2. Enter the **total amount** and the **number of parts** (2 to 60).
3. Each part shows up in the month it's due, in your budget, Upcoming and the forecast.

The plan shows its progress, for example "1 of 3 paid · €240 left".
