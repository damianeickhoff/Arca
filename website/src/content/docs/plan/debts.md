**Plan → Debts** keeps track of what you owe (a loan, a study debt, a payment arrangement) and what others owe you, and shows when each one is paid off.

## Add a debt

Use the **+** add menu → **Debt**. Choose **I owe** or **I am owed**, then fill in:

- **Name**, **Balance** and the date that balance is from (**Balance as of**).
- **Minimum monthly payment** and the **Annual interest rate**, if there is one.
- **Start date** and, optionally, the **Original debt amount** to see how far you've come.
- **Linked recurring bills**: the recurring payment that pays it off. Or switch on **Also create a recurring bill** to make one now.
- A note, an icon and a colour.

:::tip[Link the bill]
When you link a recurring bill, its payments count as progress on the debt, and its amount becomes the minimum payment. Type a different amount in **Minimum monthly payment** to override it.
:::

## The overview

At the top: **You owe** and **Owed to you**. Each debt shows its balance, how much of each payment goes to interest, and the month it's paid off. If a payment doesn't even cover the interest, Arca says **Never at this rate**.

Open a debt for its details: the linked bill, the interest in each payment, and every payment still to come. The pencil edits it.

## Payoff timeline

The **Payoff timeline** shows when you're debt-free and when each debt is done, with the months left.

## What if I pay extra?

Slide **Extra each month** to see what paying a bit more does:

- **Sooner by**: how many months earlier you're debt-free.
- **Interest saved**.
- **Free to spend each month**: what's left for you after the extra.

The extra goes to your debts in the order of the list. Sort it to choose which one gets it first: **Highest interest first**, **Smallest balance first**, **Paid off soonest** or **Largest balance first**. The payoff dates move along as you slide.

## Money owed to you

Debts others owe you, and money you advanced that's only partly paid back, are listed under **Owed to you**. Choose **Mark as settled** once it's done; any recurring payments linked to it stop from today.
