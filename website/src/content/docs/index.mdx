## What Arca does for you

<div class="tile-grid">
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="tile__chip tile__chip--mint">See where you stand</span>
    <h3>One calm number</h3>
    <p>Safe to spend is your budget minus what you've spent and the bills still to come. Home shows it first, with the sum underneath.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="tile__chip tile__chip--sky">No busywork</span>
    <h3>Transactions come in by themselves</h3>
    <p>Connect your bank, or drop in a CSV export. Rules and merchants file most transactions for you, and Arca remembers what you teach it.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="tile__chip tile__chip--lilac">Plan ahead</span>
    <h3>Budget, bills, debts and goals</h3>
    <p>Split your income into needs, wants and savings, see every bill before it lands, and watch debts shrink and goals fill up.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="tile__chip tile__chip--butter">Look ahead</span>
    <h3>A forecast that warns you</h3>
    <p>See where your balance goes over the next 30 days or 3 months, and get a heads-up before you run short.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="tile__chip tile__chip--peach">Yours</span>
    <h3>On your own server</h3>
    <p>One small database in one folder. No cloud account, no tracking. Several people in a household, two-factor sign-in and an app lock.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="tile">
    <span class="tile__chip tile__chip--rose">Everywhere</span>
    <h3>Desktop and phone</h3>
    <p>Light and dark, English and Dutch. Add it to your phone's home screen and it opens like an app.</p>
  </div>
</div>
