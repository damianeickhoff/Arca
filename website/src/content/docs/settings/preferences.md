How Arca looks, speaks and counts.

## Appearance

Settings → **Appearance**: **Light**, **Dark** or **Same as your device** (follows your device's light or dark mode). On a computer, the theme button at the bottom of the sidebar switches too: each tap goes light → dark → system. The choice is per device.

## Language

Settings → **Language**: English or Dutch (Nederlands).

## Currency

Settings → **Currency**: Euro, US dollar or British pound. This only changes the symbol shown next to amounts; nothing is converted.

## Financial month

Settings → **Financial month** sets the day your month starts, usually payday. Budgets, insights and Safe to spend all follow this period.

- Tap a day from 1 to 28 to set your start. It saves straight away, and the bar below shows where today sits in the period.
- **Move the start to Friday when it falls on a weekend**: for a salary that arrives before the weekend.
- **Exceptions**: when payday moves for just one month (holidays), add an override for that month only.

:::tip[Paid on the 25th?]
Set your start day to 25. Your "month" then runs from the 25th to the 24th, so this month's salary pays for this month's spending.
:::

## Budget and bills

Settings → **Budget & bills** decides what counts as spending.

**Count recurring bills in category budgets**

- **Always**: every bill counts toward its category's budget.
- **Only budgeted categories**: bills count only where you've set a budget for that category.
- **Never**: budgets only track day-to-day spending; recurring bills are left out.

**Shared costs**: how split bills and money paid back show up in your reports.

- **Show the full amount** (the default): reports show what left your account.
- **Show my share**: reports show only your part of a [shared cost](/Arca/everyday/activity/#shared-costs). Your cash flow and the forecast always use the full amount.
