title: Categories and groups
description: How your transactions are grouped, and what counts as needs, wants or savings.
---

Categories say what a transaction was for. Groups add categories up, like everything for the house. Find both in Settings → **Categories**.

## Groups

A **group** (Housing, Transport, Groceries…) holds categories and adds up everything inside them. In [Insights](/Arca/insights/overview/) you can switch between spending by group and by category.

Each group **counts as**:

- **Spending**: money going out. It counts towards your spending and budget.
- **Savings**: money you put aside. It's left out of your spending.
- **Income**: money coming in, like your salary.

A group also has a colour (every category in it uses that colour) and a budget type that new categories in it start with. Tap a group to fold it, or its pencil to edit it. A group can only be deleted once it's empty.

## Categories

Choose **New category** (or the **+** add menu → **Category**) and set:

- **Name**, **Icon** and the **Group** it belongs to.
- **Budget type**: **Needs**, **Wants** or **Savings**. This decides which part of your [budget strategy](/Arca/plan/budget/#your-strategy-needs-wants-and-savings) it counts towards.
- **Rules**: which transactions go here automatically. See [Rules and merchants](/Arca/transactions/rules-and-merchants/#rules).

Each category shows how many rules it has and what it spent this month.

### Choosing an icon

Tap the icon to open the icon picker (the same one for categories, merchants, goals, debts and accounts). The **Icons** tab starts with a **Suggested** row: icons that fit the name, then a hand-picked set for money things. Under it, **All icons** holds the full Tabler set of more than 5,000 icons. Tap a category chip (Food, Vehicles, Health…) to browse one kind, or type in the search field: it matches icon names and their tags (search in English for the full set; the suggested icons also answer to Dutch words). **Line** and **Filled** switch the style for icons that have a filled version. The **Brands** tab holds brand logos and your own uploads.

:::caution[Deleting a category]
Deleting a category also deletes its transactions, recurring items, budget and rules. To keep the transactions, move them to another category first (select them in Activity and change the category).
:::

## Reset to defaults

**Reset to defaults** deletes all your own categories and rules and brings back the standard set. Your transactions are kept, but lose their category until the default rules file them again.
