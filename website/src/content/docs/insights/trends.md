**Insights → Trends** shows how things change over time, per budget month.

## The cards

- Your **average income**, **average expenses** and **average balance** per month over the period, and the **total expenses**.
- **Income vs expenses**: each budget month side by side.
- **Needs and wants per month**: each month's spending split into needs (black) and wants (orange), based on your categories' [budget types](/Arca/settings/categories/). The dashed line is the needs share your [strategy](/Arca/plan/budget/#your-strategy-needs-wants-and-savings) aims for.
- **Month comparison**: two months next to each other, per category. **Change months** picks which ones.
- **Expenses per category**: a small chart per category, with its average per month and total. Filter on all, needs or wants.

Use the **Period** menu at the top to look at a longer or shorter stretch.
