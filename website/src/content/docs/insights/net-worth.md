title: Net worth
description: What you own minus what you owe, over time.
---

**Insights → Net worth** adds up everything you own and subtracts what you owe.

## The chart

Your net worth over the last **30**, **90** or **180** days. Arca keeps one snapshot per day, so the line grows from the day you start: come back tomorrow for the first comparison.

## What counts

- **Your accounts**: the balances of your bank accounts. See [Accounts and assets](/Arca/settings/accounts/).
- **Assets**: savings accounts, investments and things you own (a car, a house) that you add yourself under Accounts → **Add asset**. Switch off **Include in net worth** for anything you'd rather leave out.
- **Debts** from [Plan → Debts](/Arca/plan/debts/), with their debt-free date.
- **Savings goals** from [Plan → Goals](/Arca/plan/goals/).

The **What you own and owe** list shows each item, filtered by **Cash**, **Investments** or **Things you own**, and where it comes from: your imports, entered by you, or from Plan.

## Looking ahead

The projection estimates where your net worth goes in the coming months, from your recurring income, savings and repayments owed to you.
