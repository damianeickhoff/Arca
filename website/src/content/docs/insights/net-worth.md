**Insights → Net worth** adds up everything you own and subtracts what you owe.

## The chart

Your net worth over the last **30**, **90** or **180** days. Arca keeps one snapshot per day, so the line grows from the day you start: come back tomorrow for the first comparison.

## What counts

- **Your accounts**: the balances of your bank accounts. See [Accounts](/Arca/settings/accounts/).
- **Assets**: savings accounts, investments and things you own (a car, a house) that you add yourself under Accounts → **Add account** → **Kept by hand**. Switch off **Include in net worth** for anything you'd rather leave out.
- **Debts** from [Plan → Debts](/Arca/plan/debts/), with their debt-free date.
- **Savings goals** from [Plan → Goals](/Arca/plan/goals/).

The **What you own and owe** list shows each item, filtered by **Cash**, **Investments** or **Things you own**, and where it comes from: your imports, entered by you, or from Plan.

## Looking ahead

The projection estimates where your net worth goes in the coming months, the same way the [Forecast](/Arca/insights/forecast/) plan does: your recurring income minus what you plan to spend (your budget and the bills outside it). Money you put into savings stays yours, and paying off a debt doesn't lower your net worth (only its interest does). A repayment someone makes to you turns what you're owed into cash, so it doesn't change it either.
