Arca is a personal finance app you run on your own computer or home server. It tells you what's safe to spend today, what's coming up, and where your money went.

![Arca's Home page: safe to spend, this month in numbers and the budget](../../../assets/screenshots/home.png)

## The idea

Most money apps show you everything at once. Arca starts with one answer: **how much can I still spend this period without getting into trouble?** Everything else (budgets, bills, the forecast) feeds that answer, and is there when you want to look closer.

## What's in it

- **Home**: safe to spend, this month's money in and out, what's coming up and your accounts.
- **Activity**: every transaction, searchable, with splits, receipts, shared costs and transfers.
- **Plan**: your budget, debts and savings goals.
- **Insights**: any period in numbers, trends, net worth, a cash-flow forecast and a health score.
- **Getting transactions in**: a [bank connection](/Arca/transactions/bank-connection/) for Dutch banks, or [CSV import](/Arca/transactions/csv-import/) for any bank.

## Who it's for

Arca is made for people who like to keep their own data at home, on a NAS, an Unraid server or any machine that runs Docker. You don't need to be a developer: if you can add a container, you can run Arca.

- **Your data stays with you.** Everything lives in one folder on your server. There's no Arca cloud and no account with us.
- **For the whole household.** Several people can sign in to the same household and share accounts, budgets and goals.
- **Works on your phone.** Arca adapts to small screens and can be added to your home screen like an app.

:::note[Work in progress]
Arca is actively developed and gets frequent updates. Now and then something changes in a bigger way. [Back up your data](/Arca/getting-started/updating-and-backups/) before you update.
:::
