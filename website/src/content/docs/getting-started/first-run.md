title: First run

---

The first time you open Arca, a short setup creates your account and your household. It takes about five minutes, and you can skip anything and come back to it later.

## The steps

1. **Welcome.** A quick look at what Arca does. Choose **Get started**.
2. **About you.** Your first and last name, your email address (you sign in with it) and, if you like, your birthday.
3. **Password.** At least 8 characters. A strength meter and a short checklist help you pick a good one.
4. **Two-factor sign-in.** Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator or any other) and type the 6-digit code. From then on you're asked for a code when you sign in on a new device.
5. **Backup codes.** Ten codes that each sign you in once if you lose your phone. Download or copy them and keep them somewhere safe.
6. **Photo and language.** A profile photo (optional) and English or Dutch.
7. **Make it yours.** Light, dark, or the same as your device.
8. **Budget strategy.** How your income is split over needs, wants and savings. 50 / 30 / 20 works for most households; you can pick another preset or set your own.
9. **Your first bill.** Rent, energy, a subscription: Arca will expect it every month. Quick picks fill in the name.
10. **Import transactions.** Upload a CSV export from your bank. ING, Rabobank, ABN AMRO, bunq, Revolut and Knab are recognised automatically.
11. **App lock.** A 4 to 6 digit PIN that's asked whenever Arca opens.
12. **All set.** A summary of what was set up. Choose **Open Arca**.

:::tip[Skip what you're not sure about]
Every step except your name, email and password can be skipped with **Skip for now**. Two-factor sign-in, the app lock, bills and imports can all be set up later from Settings or the add menu.
:::

## The first account is the admin

The person who goes through the setup first becomes the **admin** of the household. Admins can manage who signs in; see [Sign-in and security](/Arca/settings/security/#people-in-your-household).

## Other people in your household

Everyone else creates their own account from the sign-in page with **New here? Create an account**. They join the same household as a **member**: they see and use the same accounts, budgets and goals, but can't manage users.

## After the setup

Home shows a **Get started** checklist until you have some history:

- Import a bank CSV (or [connect your bank](/Arca/transactions/bank-connection/))
- Set a monthly budget
- Add at least one recurring bill
- Create a savings goal

Arca also explains new things the first time you see them, with a small tip card next to the feature. Tap **Got it** to close one, or **Don't show again** to stop them. Tips for features added later still show once.
